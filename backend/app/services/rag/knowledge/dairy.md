# Dairy Business Compliance Requirements in India

Covers all licensing and registration requirements for micro-dairy enterprises
including milk collection, chilling, processing, value-added products (paneer,
ghee, curd), and retail sale.

## FSSAI Licence (Food Safety)

**Authority:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
**Portal:** foscos.fssai.gov.in

For micro-dairies with annual turnover < ₹12 lakh: FSSAI **Registration** (not Licence).
For turnover ₹12 lakh–₹20 crore: State FSSAI Licence.
For turnover > ₹20 crore: Central FSSAI Licence.

Most rural micro-dairy units qualify for Basic Registration:
- Fee: ₹100/year
- Issued by designated officer at the Panchayat / Block level
- Valid for 1 to 5 years

Required for:
- Any handling, processing, storage, or sale of milk and dairy products
- Transport of milk in any quantity

State-specific: Bihar State Milk Cooperative Federation (COMFED) units may
have simplified registration pathways. Rajasthan dairies: check local FSSAI
Divisional Office.

**Required: YES — FSSAI Registration or Licence.**

## Prevention of Food Adulteration (PFA) Compliance

Dairy businesses must comply with PFA Act standards:
- Fat and SNF (Solid Not Fat) content standards for milk
- No addition of water, preservatives, or adulterants
- Proper labelling for packaged products

**Required: YES (compliance, not a separate registration).**

## Udyam Registration

See base_licenses.md. Required for:
- PMEGP subsidy on dairy equipment (chilling plant, separator)
- Priority sector credit at concessional rates

**Required: YES.**

## Trade Licence

See base_licenses.md. For dairy stalls or outlets at mandis, the local
Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) NOC may also be required.

**Required: YES.**

## Milk Collection / Chilling Plant NOC

For mini dairy plants with chilling capacity > 1000 litres/day:
- State Dairy Development Department NOC
- Applicable states: Bihar, UP, Punjab, Haryana, Maharashtra

Bihar-specific: Bihar State Dairy Development Corporation approval for
any unit receiving milk from > 5 farmer households.

**Required: Conditional (if capacity > 1000 L/day).**

## BIS Certification (Packaged Products)

If selling packaged milk (pouches, bottles) or processed dairy (butter, ghee):
- Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) mark required for standardised products
- IS 1479 for cow milk, IS 1167 for ghee

**Required: Conditional (only for branded/packaged products).**

## Vehicle Permit (Milk Transportation)

For transporting milk across state boundaries:
- National Permit / State Goods Vehicle Permit
- Reefer vehicle certification for chilled transport

**Required: Conditional (inter-state transport only).**

## Environmental Compliance

For dairy plants with effluent discharge (wash water, whey):
- State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) Consent to Establish (CTE)
- Bihar SPCB: units processing > 500 litres/day require CTE/CTO

**Required: Conditional (units > 500 L/day processing).**

## Regional Notes — Bihar

- Jeevika (Bihar Rural Livelihoods Mission) SHG-linked dairies: FSSAI registration
  can be done in bulk through BRLPS facilitation centre
- Milk collection centres in Mithilanchal region: Samastipur and Darbhanga DIC
  offices process Udyam + FSSAI simultaneously
- PM FME (Formalisation of Micro Food Enterprises) scheme: covers 50% of eligible
  project cost for dairy units up to ₹10 lakh; Udyam + FSSAI mandatory

## Regional Notes — Maharashtra

- MahaFPC (Maharashtra Farmer Producer Companies): bulk FSSAI registration available
- Additional: Maharashtra Animal Husbandry Department NOC for bulk milk procurement

## Regional Notes — Punjab / Haryana

- Punjab Milk Federation (MILKFED) empanelment may replace FSSAI Basic Registration
  for cooperative-linked units
