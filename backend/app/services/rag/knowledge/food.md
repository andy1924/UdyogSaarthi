# Food Business Compliance Requirements in India

Covers restaurants, dhabas, tiffin services, snack stalls, catering units,
packaged food manufacturing, and agro-processing micro-enterprises.

## FSSAI Licence (Mandatory for All Food Businesses)

**Authority:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)
**Portal:** foscos.fssai.gov.in

Turnover tiers:
- < ₹12 lakh/year → FSSAI Basic Registration (₹100/year)
- ₹12 lakh – ₹20 crore → State Licence (₹2,000–₹5,000/year)
- > ₹20 crore → Central Licence

For roadside dhabas and tiffin centres: Basic Registration is sufficient.
For cloud kitchens selling on Zomato/Swiggy: State Licence required.

Renewal: Annual. Non-renewal attracts penalty of ₹100/day.

**Required: YES — mandatory for any food handling.**

## Trade Licence / Shop & Establishment

For food businesses operating in premises (restaurant, dhaba, shop):
- Trade Licence from local body (Gram Panchayat / Municipal)
- Shops & Establishments Act registration (if employing workers)

**Required: YES.**

## Udyam Registration

Required for PMEGP subsidy eligibility on food processing equipment
(grinder, mixer, packaging unit, cold storage).

PM FME scheme: Covers 35% of eligible project cost for food units;
FSSAI + Udyam both mandatory prerequisites.

**Required: YES.**

## Fire Safety NOC

For enclosed food establishments with seating capacity > 50 persons or
any premises using LPG cylinders > 5 commercial cylinders/month:
- Fire Safety Certificate from local fire department
- Applicable in urban/semi-urban areas

**Required: Conditional (for dine-in establishments).**

## Eating House Licence

For establishments providing food for consumption on premises:
- Issued by local police commissioner / municipality in many states
- UP: eating house rules 2017 — registration required for turnover > ₹5 lakh
- Bihar: Panchayat Raj Department issues for rural eating places

**Required: Conditional (state-specific for dine-in).**

## GST Registration

Mandatory if turnover > ₹20 lakh, or for online food delivery businesses
on aggregator platforms (mandatory regardless of turnover).

**Required: Conditional (turnover-based).**

## Packaged Food Labelling (FSSAI Regulations)

For any manufactured/packaged food sold to retail:
- Label must include: product name, ingredients, net quantity, batch no,
  best before date, nutritional info, FSSAI licence number
- Mandatory from 2022 for all packaged food including home-made units

**Required: YES (for packaged food products).**

## Agro-Processing Specific Licences

For millet processing, spice grinding, pulse dal units:
- PM FME scheme eligibility requires Udyam + FSSAI
- State-level agro-processing cluster incentives (UP, MP, Odisha):
  additional state Udyog NOC required

**Required: Conditional (for agro-processing units).**

## Regional Notes — Uttar Pradesh

UP Food Processing Mission: grants of up to ₹10 lakh for food processing
micro-units; FSSAI State Licence and Udyam both mandatory.

## Regional Notes — Bihar

BRLPS (Jeevika) Self-Help Groups: FSSAI group registration available;
one FSSAI licence can cover up to 5 SHG food units at same address.

## Regional Notes — Rajasthan

Rajasthan Agro-Processing, Agri-Business and Agri-Export Promotion Policy:
additional state-level NOC for units exporting food products.
