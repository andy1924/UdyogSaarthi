# Retail Business Compliance Requirements in India

Covers kirana stores, general merchandise retail, mobile/accessories retail,
clothing, agricultural inputs, hardware, and rural FMCG distribution.

## Trade Licence / Shop & Establishment

**Authority:** Gram Panchayat (rural), Municipal Corporation (urban)
**Applicability:** All retail premises open to the public

Requirements:
- Trade Licence from local body (Panchayat/Municipal)
- Shops & Establishments Act registration (state-specific):
  - Bihar Shops & Establishments Act: registration within 30 days of opening
  - UP Dukan evam Vanijya Aashthan Adhiniyam: mandatory for any shop
  - MP Shops & Establishments: applicable to shops with ≥ 1 employee

**Required: YES.**

## Udyam Registration

For MUDRA loans (Shishu/Kishore/Tarun tiers), DIC-referred bank credit,
and government scheme benefits.

**Required: YES.**

## GST Registration

Threshold: ₹20 lakh annual turnover (₹10 lakh for special category states).
For kirana stores selling goods across state lines: mandatory regardless of turnover.

GST Composition Scheme: Eligible for turnovers up to ₹1.5 crore. Pay flat 1%
GST instead of filing monthly returns. Suitable for small kirana stores.

**Required: Conditional (mandatory if turnover > threshold).**

## Pesticide Dealer Licence (Agricultural Inputs)

For shops selling pesticides, fertilisers, or agricultural chemicals:
- Pesticides Act 1968: Licence from State Agriculture Department
- Bihar: Block-level Agriculture Office issues dealer licence
- Annual renewal required; records of sales to be maintained

**Required: Conditional (agricultural input retailers only).**

## Drug Licence (Medical Retail)

For any shop selling OTC medicines, ayurvedic products, or regulated drugs:
- State Drug Control Authority licence
- Pharmacist qualification required for Schedule H drugs
- Bihar Drug Control: Patna office for state-level licences

**Required: Conditional (medical/pharmacy retailers).**

## Weights and Measures Certification

All retail shops using weighing scales, measuring instruments:
- Legal Metrology Act 2009 compliance
- Stamping of weighing instruments by State Weights & Measures department
- Annual verification required

**Required: YES (if using any weighing/measuring equipment).**

## APMC Licence (Agricultural Produce Markets)

For shops located within APMC mandi premises or trading in notified
agricultural commodities:
- Market Committee Licence from the APMC
- Applicable in states with active APMC Acts (Punjab, Maharashtra, Gujarat)
- Bihar, UP: APMC reformed — licence may not be needed for most commodities

**Required: Conditional (for mandi-based agricultural traders).**

## FCI / State Warehousing Corporation Empanelment

For retailers involved in PDS distribution or FCI grain procurement:
- State Food Corporation empanelment required
- Bihar State Food Corporation: separate dealer registration

**Required: Conditional (PDS/grain retail only).**

## Regional Notes — Chhattisgarh

Minor Forest Produce (MFP) dealers: CGMFP Federation licence required for
trading in tendu leaves, sal seeds, mahua, and other notified forest produce.

## Regional Notes — Jharkhand

Jharkhand Rural FMCG Distribution: JSLPS empanelment for SHG-linked retail
points; mandatory Udyam + bank account.

## Regional Notes — West Bengal

West Bengal Shops & Establishments: registration mandatory from Day 1;
penalty of ₹5,000 for non-compliance.
