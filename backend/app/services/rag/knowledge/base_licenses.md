# Base Licensing Requirements for All Indian Micro-Enterprises

This document covers the core national-level registrations applicable to all
micro-enterprises across India, regardless of business sector or state.

## Udyam Registration

**Authority:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)
**Portal:** udyamregistration.gov.in
**Applicable to:** All manufacturing and service micro-enterprises

Required for:
- Access to priority sector lending under RBI guidelines
- Eligibility for government scheme subsidies (PM SVANidhi, PMEGP, MUDRA)
- Protection under MSME Development Act 2006
- TReDS invoice discounting platform access

Micro-enterprise definition: Investment ≤ ₹1 crore AND Turnover ≤ ₹5 crore.

Process: Self-declaration via Udyam portal using Aadhaar + PAN. No documents
need to be uploaded. Certificate generated immediately.

**Required: YES for all business types.**

## Trade Licence

**Authority:** Gram Panchayat (rural), Municipal Corporation (urban)
**Also known as:** Shop & Establishment licence, Panchayat NOC

Required for:
- Commencement of any commercial activity within the jurisdiction
- Renewal annually (or as per local body schedule)

For rural Bihar, Jharkhand, UP, MP:
- Issued by the Gram Panchayat Secretary
- Fee: ₹200–₹500 depending on turnover bracket
- Required within 30 days of business commencement

**Required: YES for all business types.**

## GST Registration

**Authority:** Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN)
**Portal:** gst.gov.in
**Threshold:** Turnover > ₹20 lakh per year (₹10 lakh for special category states)
Special category states include: Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, Meghalaya,
Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, and Himachal Pradesh.

Voluntary GST registration is allowed and may be beneficial for:
- B2B transactions requiring input tax credit
- Online marketplaces (mandatory regardless of turnover)

**Required: Conditional (only if turnover threshold crossed).**

## PAN Card (Business)

Required for:
- GST registration
- Opening business bank accounts
- Income tax filing

**Required: YES (baseline for all registrations).**

## Aadhaar-Linked Bank Account

Required for:
- PMEGP subsidy disbursement
- DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) from government schemes
- PM SVANidhi digital loan disbursement

**Required: YES for scheme eligibility.**
