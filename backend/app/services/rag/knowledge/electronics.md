# Electronics Business Compliance Requirements in India

Covers mobile phone repair, electronics retail, computer hardware,
electrical accessories, battery and inverter sales, and solar equipment.

## Trade Licence / Shop & Establishment

**Authority:** Gram Panchayat (rural) or Municipal Body (urban)
**Required:** Yes — for any premises open to customers.

Shops & Establishments Act registration (state-specific):
- Bihar, UP, MP, Rajasthan: required within 30 days of opening
- Penalty for non-compliance: ₹1,000–₹5,000 (state-specific)

**Required: YES.**

## Udyam Registration

For MUDRA loans, DIC-referenced credit, and scheme eligibility (PMEGP).
Electronics micro-units may also qualify for PLI (Production Linked Incentive)
scheme benefits — Udyam registration is the baseline credential.

**Required: YES.**

## GST Registration

All electronics goods attract GST (typically 12–18%); most electronics
retailers cross the ₹20 lakh turnover threshold quickly.

For mobile phone repair shops: GST on services (18% for repair services).

**Required: YES (strongly recommended for all electronics businesses).**

## E-Waste Management Authorisation

**Authority:** State Pollution Control Board (SPCB)
**Applicable law:** E-Waste (Management) Rules 2016 (amended 2022)

For shops handling consumer electronics (mobile phones, computers, TVs):
- Bulk consumer registration if disposing > 250 kg/year of e-waste
- Must hand over e-waste only to authorised recyclers/dismantlers
- Bihar SPCB: authorisation from Patna regional office

For repair shops: maintain an e-waste disposal register.

**Required: YES — compliance with disposal rules; authorisation if bulk.**

## BIS Certification (Import / Manufacturing)

For any business importing or manufacturing electronics:
- Bureau of Indian Standards mandatory certification (BIS IS 13252)
- Applies to power adaptors, laptops, mobile chargers, LED lights
- Import of non-BIS certified goods: prohibited from 2022

For retail-only (no manufacturing/import): not directly required, but
stock only BIS-certified goods to avoid liability.

**Required: Conditional (importers and manufacturers).**

## Electrical Contractor Licence

For businesses providing electrical installation, wiring, or solar panel
installation services:
- State Electrical Inspectorate licence (Electricity Act 2003)
- Wireman/Supervisor grade certificate from ITI required
- Bihar: Bihar Electricity Regulatory Commission contractor registration

**Required: Conditional (electrical installation services).**

## Solar Energy Business (MNRE Empanelment)

For solar panel/equipment dealers and installation service providers:
- Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) empanelment
- DISCOM (distribution company) empanelment for grid-tied systems
- PM Surya Ghar scheme: empanelled installers only eligible for subsidy jobs

**Required: Conditional (solar equipment business).**

## Consumer Protection Compliance

Electronics retailers must comply with:
- Consumer Protection Act 2019: valid warranty cards, no misleading claims
- BIS Act 2016: display of mandatory BIS certification on products
- Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules: for packaged accessories

**Required: YES (compliance; no separate registration).**

## Regional Notes — Bihar

Bihar Electronics Manufacturing Cluster (Bihta, Patna): cluster-based
Udyam registration available; DIC Patna provides facilitation.

## Regional Notes — Rajasthan

Rajasthan Electronics Policy 2022: subsidy on capital investment for
electronics repair micro-units — Udyam mandatory prerequisite.

## Regional Notes — Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu Electronics Hardware Policy: state-level NOC from TIDCO for
electronics manufacturing units above ₹10 lakh investment.
